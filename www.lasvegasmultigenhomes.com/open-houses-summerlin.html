How to Prepare for an Open House Visit

To make the most of your open house experience in Summerlin, follow these expert tips:

Do Your Research First

Before attending, review the property listing, check comparable sales in the neighborhood, and understand what similar homes are selling for. This preparation helps you ask informed questions and recognize a good value when you see it.

Take Notes and Photos

After visiting several open houses, homes can blend together in your memory. Take photos (ask permission first), jot down notes about what you liked and didn't like, and document any questions that arise. This information becomes invaluable when making your final decision.

Bring Your Whole Family

For multi-generational homes, it's crucial that everyone who will be living there attends the open house. Different generations have different needs—let aging parents assess accessibility, let kids check out their potential rooms, and ensure everyone feels comfortable with the layout.

Test the Commute

Since you're already in the neighborhood, take time to drive to important locations—work, schools, medical facilities, shopping, and recreation areas. Understanding commute times and accessibility helps you make a more informed decision.
