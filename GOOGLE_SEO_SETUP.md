## 📋 NEXT STEPS - Submit to Google

### Step 1: Google Search Console Setup
1. Go to: https://search.google.com/search-console/
2. Click "Add Property"
3. Enter: `https://www.lasvegasmultigenhomes.com`
4. **Verification Options:**
   - **Recommended**: HTML file upload verification
   - **Alternative**: Add meta tag to `<head>` section
   - **Easiest**: Use your existing Google Analytics (if you have access)

### Step 2: Submit Your Sitemap
Once verified in Search Console:
1. Go to "Sitemaps" in the left menu
2. Add new sitemap URL: `https://www.lasvegasmultigenhomes.com/sitemap.xml`
3. Click "Submit"
4. Google will start crawling within 24-48 hours

### Step 3: Request Indexing (Optional but Recommended)
1. In Google Search Console, go to "URL Inspection"
2. Enter your homepage URL: `https://www.lasvegasmultigenhomes.com`
3. Click "Request Indexing"
4. Repeat for key pages:
   - `https://www.lasvegasmultigenhomes.com/tour.html`
   - `https://www.lasvegasmultigenhomes.com/dual-master-suite-homes-las-vegas.html`

### Step 4: Google Business Profile (Recommended)
1. Go to: https://www.google.com/business/
2. Create/claim your business listing
3. Add your website URL
4. Verify your business location (if applicable)
5. This helps with local SEO in Las Vegas area

### Step 5: Monitor Performance
After submission, monitor in Google Search Console:
- **Coverage**: Check for any indexing errors
- **Performance**: Track impressions, clicks, and rankings
- **Mobile Usability**: Ensure your site is mobile-friendly
- **Core Web Vitals**: Monitor page speed and user experience
