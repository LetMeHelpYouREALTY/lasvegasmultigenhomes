## 🎓 LESSONS LEARNED

### **What Was Wrong:**
1. **Multiple H1 tags** confuse Google about page topic
2. **Missing H1 tags** make page seem incomplete to Google
3. **No alt text** hurts accessibility and image SEO
4. **Missing rel="noopener"** is security vulnerability

### **Why It Matters:**
- Google uses **H1 tags** to understand page topic
- **Only 1 H1** tells Google exactly what page is about
- **Multiple H1s** confuse ranking signals
- **Alt text** helps Google Images and accessibility
- **rel="noopener"** protects users from malicious sites
