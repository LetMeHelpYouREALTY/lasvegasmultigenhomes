## 📋 Alt Text Best Practices

### ✅ DO:
1. **Be Descriptive** - Explain what's in the image
2. **Include Keywords** - Natural use of "Summerlin", "multi-generational", "dual master suite"
3. **Be Specific** - "Primary master suite" vs just "bedroom"
4. **Keep Reasonable Length** - 125 characters or less (screen reader friendly)
5. **Include Location** - "West Summerlin", "Las Vegas"
6. **Highlight Features** - "with fireplace", "with walk-in closet"

### ❌ DON'T:
1. **Keyword Stuff** - Don't repeat keywords unnaturally
2. **Use "Image of" or "Photo of"** - Screen readers already announce it's an image
3. **Be Too Generic** - "Nice house" doesn't help anyone
4. **Leave Empty** - Every image needs alt text
5. **Use File Names** - "IMG_1234.jpg" is not descriptive
6. **Be Too Long** - Over 200 characters is too much

## 🎨 Alt Text Formula

### For Property Photos:
**[Room/Area] + [Key Features] + [Location/Context]**

Examples:
- "Primary master suite with ensuite bathroom and walk-in closet"
- "Updated kitchen with island and modern appliances in West Summerlin home"
- "Spacious backyard with covered patio ready for pool installation"

### For Agent Photos:
**[Name] + [Role/Specialty] + [Location]**

Examples:
- "Dr. Jan Duffy - Summerlin West Real Estate Agent"
- "Dr. Jan Duffy - Las Vegas Multi-Generational Homes Specialist"

### For Exterior Photos:
**[View Type] + [Address/Location] + [Neighborhood]**

Examples:
- "Front exterior of dual master suite home at 11312 Parkside Way in Vistas at West Summerlin Las Vegas"
- "Curb view of 4-bedroom home in Summerlin North Las Vegas"
