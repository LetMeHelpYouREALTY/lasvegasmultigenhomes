## 🎯 SEO Best Practices

### Heading Hierarchy Rules
1. **Exactly ONE H1 per page** - Primary keyword target
2. **Multiple H2 tags** - Main section headers (3-6 per page)
3. **Multiple H3 tags** - Subsections under H2s
4. **H4-H6** - Use sparingly for deep hierarchy

### Word Count Guidelines
- **Minimum**: 750 words
- **Optimal**: 1,000-1,500 words for main pages
- **Maximum**: 2,500 words (beyond this, split into multiple pages)

## 📝 Content Writing Guidelines

### For All Pages:

1. **Introduction (150-200 words)**
   - Hook with primary keyword
   - Establish expertise
   - Preview what reader will learn

2. **Main Body (500-1,000 words)**
   - H2 sections with clear topics
   - H3 subsections for details
   - Bullet points for scannability
   - Include statistics and facts
   - Use location keywords naturally

3. **Call-to-Action Section (100-150 words)**
   - Strong CTA with keywords
   - Multiple contact options
   - Sense of urgency

4. **FAQ or Additional Info (optional)**
   - Address common questions
   - Long-tail keyword opportunities

### Writing Style:
- **Conversational but professional**
- **Short paragraphs** (2-4 sentences)
- **Active voice**
- **Include local references** (Red Rock Canyon, Downtown Summerlin, etc.)
- **Buyer-focused** (benefits, not just features)
