## 🎓 Internal Linking Best Practices

### DO:
✅ Link to related, relevant content  
✅ Use descriptive, keyword-rich anchor text  
✅ Link from high-authority pages to new pages  
✅ Create a logical site hierarchy  
✅ Link early in content (first 100 words)  
✅ Use a mix of exact match and partial match anchors  
✅ Make links contextual and natural  
✅ Update old posts with links to new content  

### DON'T:
❌ Over-optimize with exact match keywords  
❌ Use generic "click here" anchor text  
❌ Create circular linking patterns  
❌ Link to every page from every page  
❌ Use the same anchor text repeatedly  
❌ Force links where they don't fit naturally  
❌ Forget to update broken links  
❌ Ignore link juice flow (orphan pages)
